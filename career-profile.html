Why this role, why MIT Sloan

For six and a half years at Harvard FAS I wrote SQL, Python, R and Crystal Reports for more than 50 departments, and ad-hoc requests fell from 300 to 400 a year to under 100 once reusable reports went live. Before that I spent nearly eleven years on gift records and campaign reporting at UAMS and Phillips Academy. This role asks for both: Annual Fund reporting, segmentation and suppression logic, forecasting, and the discipline to keep OER and central numbers in agreement. MIT Sloan’s Annual Fund raised $5.7 million from 2,452 donors in FY25, and 680 alumni and friends gave on the 2026 MIT 24-Hour Challenge. Numbers like these deserve one definition everyone uses. That is the work I want to do.
