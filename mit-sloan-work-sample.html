Straight answers

  • Annual Fund. I have not owned an Annual Fund. The closest I have come is gift, campaign and board reporting at Phillips Academy and UAMS, where annual giving was part of every report. The Annual Fund views here show how I would learn OER’s, not a claim that I ran one.
  • Since November 2025. I have worked full time in my independent practice, building report templates and data and AI rollout plans for advancement and nonprofit clients.
  • Title. At Harvard my title was IT Reporting and Data Analyst Professional, and I served as data owner for FAS Faculty Affairs.
  • Tools. I have not used SAS, SPSS, Cognos or Salesforce Marketing Cloud in production. I do the same work in R, Python, SQL, Tableau and Power BI, and I would learn OER’s tools first on its existing reports.
  • How I built this. With AI assistance, as a deliberate demonstration of faster analysis. I chose every method, checked every number against its source, and can defend each one line by line.

How I handle donor data

  • Least privilege. Access only to the fields a report needs, reviewed when a project ends.
  • Personal data in extracts. Constituent IDs instead of names where possible, no home addresses or birthdates unless the request needs them, and extracts deleted on a set date.
  • Secure transfer. MIT-approved shared drives and vendor portals, never email attachments of donor lists.
  • Audit trail. Every list and report saved with its query, date, requester and row count, so it can be rebuilt.
  • No donor data in samples. Everything in this work sample is synthetic.
